ShiftShield: Continual Learning for Cyberattack Detection

Pick the attacks that will hit the network, press Start, then feed traffic in batches. Attack types arrive one after another and traffic drift simulates real-world distribution shift. Two identical detectors watch the same flows: ShiftShield (with reservoir replay) preserves memory of past attack types, while the naive model experiences catastrophic forgetting.

🟢 Active Dataset: CIC-IDS2017 (Real Flows) Real-world network attack packet flows loaded directly from MachineLearningCSV
